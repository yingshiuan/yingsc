#### My Journey in Design & Tech

I started as an architect in Taiwan, designing spaces that connect people. But I kept gravitating toward the digital side: computational design, digital and robotic fabrication, programming robotic arms to build physical structures. That fascination led me to ETH Zürich for a [Master's in Architecture and Digital Fabrication](https://masdfab.arch.ethz.ch/), where I discovered I loved building digital tools as much as physical spaces.

Since then, I’ve transitioned fully into software engineering and interaction design. I’ve built AR/VR prototypes for motion tracking at [Sensoryx](https://sensoryx.tech)<span class="material-symbols-outlined">view_in_ar</span>, optimized e-commerce platforms UX (+35% sales)<span class="material-symbols-outlined">web</span>, and developed web applications and data visualization tools at [DisneyResearch|Studios](https://studios.disneyresearch.com).<span class="material-symbols-outlined">computer</span>
